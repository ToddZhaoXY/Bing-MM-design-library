How to use

  1. Explore variants

    Choose Desktop or Mobile, then switch the available layouts, content options, and states.

  2. Inspect the details

    Use Anatomy to identify each part, and Properties to review available values and default content.

  3. Reuse a pattern

    Review the Example, copy the available SDL or HTML, and open the linked Mosaic template for its source.
